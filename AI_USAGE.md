# AI Usage

* **Part A (Tokenizer Audit):** AI helped me quickly draft the Python scripts (`prepare_corpus.py`, `test_bugs.py`, `test_sentence.py`, and `corrected_analysis.py`). When downloading FLORES-200, the AI suggested using the `datasets` library, but we ran into an issue where modern versions of `datasets` blocked remote scripts, and then a Windows encoding issue (`charmap codec`). The AI guided me through downgrading the library and using the `Muennighoff/flores200` mirror to successfully pull the dataset. The AI also helped me articulate the conceptual flaw regarding tokens/word vs tokens/sentence.
* **Part B (Capacity Reconciliation):** AI was used as a calculator and sounding board. I asked it to multiply out the KV cache bytes, and it verified my math (114,688 bytes per token). It also helped me quickly write a calculation to derive generated-token goodput (200.9 tok/s) from the benchmark log.
* **Part C (Decision Memo):** I brainstormed the constraints with the AI. The AI helped me compare the trade-offs between prompt engineering, an inference-time rewriter, and parameter-efficient SFT under the stated constraints. I wrote the final memo structure based on our brainstorming session.
